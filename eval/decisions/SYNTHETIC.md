# SYNTHETIC DATA

Everything in this directory is invented and written by hand for the decision eval of Memini.

- The threads and meetings under `cases/`, the people in them (the cast of `eval/comms/people.json`)
  and the decisions they discuss are fictional; nothing is copied from or derived from a real
  conversation, project or company.
- The labels say which items each record holds; they describe these invented records only.
- The baselines (`baseline-*.json`) are reports of `memini eval decisions` over these sets.
- No real person, company, message, credential, token or key.

Keep it that way: a change to this directory adds invented content only (CLAUDE.md, "Never commit").
