# Glossary

The code and the reports of the bakery use Russian words; this is what they mean.

## Starter

Zakvaska: a culture of flour and water kept alive by feeding it every day. Rye bread rises on it
instead of on yeast, which gives the crumb its sour taste.

The starter for the next day is set in the evening; how much depends on the flour of tomorrow's
doughs and on the temperature in the bakery.

## Proofing

Rasstoika: the last rest of the shaped dough before it goes into the oven. It happens in a warm,
humid cabinet, so that the crust does not dry out.

Underproofed loaves tear open in the oven; overproofed ones collapse.

## Defects

Brak: loaves that fail the inspection, whether burnt, underbaked or misshapen. They are counted
in the daily report as a share of the output and never sold.

A defect rate above five per cent on one product is a reason to check its oven and its recipe.
