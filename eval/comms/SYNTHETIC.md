# SYNTHETIC DATA

Everything in this directory is invented and written by hand for the tests and the evals of Memini.

- No real person, company, product, place, chat, mailbox, meeting or message appears here, and
  nothing here is derived from real data.
- Saltreed Harbourworks, the Port of Hollin Mere, Fogbell Instruments, Tidewater, Beacon, the RX-40
  receiver and every person in `people.json` are fictional.
- Addresses and links use reserved domains only: `*.example.test` and `*.example.org` (RFC 2606).
  Chat user ids, channel ids, message ids and meeting ids are made up.
- No credentials, tokens or keys.

Keep it that way: a change to this directory adds invented content only (CLAUDE.md, "Never commit").
