# SYNTHETIC DATA

Everything in this directory is invented and written by hand for the domain eval of Memini.

- `set.json`: the catalog of projects and domains, the decisions and the messages they cite, and
  the labels saying which domains each decision belongs to, are fictional; nothing is copied from
  or derived from a real conversation, project or company.
- The baselines (`baseline-*.json`) are reports of `memini eval domains` over this set.
- No real person, company, message, credential, token or key.

Keep it that way: a change to this directory adds invented content only (CLAUDE.md, "Never commit").
