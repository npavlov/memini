# Scenario template

Copy this file to `SNN-short-name.md`, replace the first line with `# SNN. The question` (the
number without a leading zero, as in `# S15. ...`), and fill every part. Keep the table and the
headings as they are: the index and its test read them.

| Area | Who | Status | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| One of the areas of the index | Who has the pain | works, partial, planned or to confirm | The issue, or none |

## Who and pain

The person, the situation, and what hurts today. One or two sentences.

## Question

The question in the person's own words, with invented names.

## Answer

The tool or route, its filters, and what the answer contains. Design the API here.

## Data

What must be in the base to answer, who puts it there and by which path: git sync, a push from
outside, the catalog, a derive job. If the data cannot get in, the scenario is not ready.

## Check

The eval question or the acceptance test that proves the answer, on synthetic fixtures.
